  • Electrical/Thermal Load Profile(MATLAB)

    Aug 2021 – Jan 2023

    In this project, we developed a comprehensive load profiling methodology for combined electrical and thermal demands in off-grid rural settings. We collected real-world consumption data via smart meters and temperature sensors, then applied statistical clustering and time-series analysis to categorize typical daily and seasonal load behaviors. The electrical profile informed PV and battery sizing, while the thermal profile guided solar thermal collector and heat storage design. Results demonstrated up to a 25% improvement in system efficiency and a 30% reduction in energy shortfall compared to conventional sizing methods.
